Levora Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

Levora is a reading app. It lets you read PDF books, translate selected text, listen to narration, save quotes and words, and create shareable cards. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: your books and notes stay on your device, there is no account, and there are no ads or analytics.

1. What stays on your device

The following is stored only on your phone and is never sent to the developer:

  • Your books and PDF files, covers, ratings, reviews and reading progress
  • Saved quotes, translation history, vocabulary words and flashcards
  • Reading streaks, challenges, badges, profile name and photo, and app settings
  • Usage counters for free plan limits (for example, translations used today)

Uninstalling the app removes this data. We cannot access it, so we cannot recover it for you.

2. Services that receive limited information

Some features need an internet connection and contact third party services. We do not control these services. What they receive:

FeatureServiceWhat is sent
TranslationMyMemory (translated.net) and the public Google Translate endpointThe text you select to translate (up to about 4,500 characters), the target language, and your IP address. No name or account information.
AI translation (optional)AnthropicOnly if you enter your own Anthropic API key in the app. The selected text is sent using your key. Without a key this is never used.
Scanning photographed pages (OCR)Tesseract.js, loaded from the jsDelivr networkThe page image is read on your device and is not uploaded. Downloading the engine and language data reveals your IP address to the content network.
FontsGoogle FontsA font request, which includes your IP address.
Free launch offerGoogle Firebase Realtime DatabaseA random, anonymous identifier created on your first launch, used only to count the first 200 users. It contains no name, email or device information, and Google receives your IP address with the request.
SubscriptionsGoogle Play and RevenueCatPurchase and subscription status, an anonymous app user identifier, and purchase tokens. Payment details are handled by Google Play and are never visible to us.

Narration uses the text to speech engine built into your device.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, email address or phone number.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.

4. Subscriptions and promo codes

Levora Pro is billed by Google Play and renews automatically until you cancel. You can manage or cancel it at any time in Google Play, under Payments and subscriptions. Promo codes and the launch offer are checked inside the app and grant Pro for a limited time without any payment.

5. Sharing images

When you save or share a quote or book card, the image is created on your device and handed to the app you choose. What you do with it afterwards is under your control and the policy of that app.

6. Your choices and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you control it. You can delete individual books, quotes and words inside the app, or remove everything by clearing the app storage or uninstalling. To ask about the anonymous launch offer identifier, contact us using the address below.

7. Children

Levora is a general audience reading app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

8. Security

Connections to the services above use HTTPS. Your library is kept in your device's private app storage.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.
